Jump back to the thread you were just in, then forward again, with
`Mod+[` and `Mod+]`.

## What you get

- Back and forward through the threads you opened in this window, in the order
  you opened them, like browser history.
- The same two moves in the quick palette as "Back to previous thread" and
  "Forward to next thread".

## How it works

The plugin remembers up to 50 threads per window and keeps that list across a
reload. Going back after opening a new thread discards the forward entries.
Nothing leaves the machine and there is nothing to configure.
